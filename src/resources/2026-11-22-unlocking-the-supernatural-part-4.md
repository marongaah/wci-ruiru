## Unlocking the Supernatural by the Power of Faith
*Mark 9:23 and John 14:12*

> If thou canst believe, all things are possible to him that believeth. (Mark 9:23)

> He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do. (John 14:12)

## But what is faith?

1. Faith is key to everything that redemption offers. *(Revelation 5:12, Matthew 9:29)*
2. Faith is not waiting for God to work. It is putting God's word to work, so as to commit God to make good His promise. *(Philippians 2:12, 2 Corinthians 6:2, Proverbs 3:27-28, James 2:14-26)*
3. Faith is taking full spiritual responsibility in the light of the scriptures, thereby committing God's integrity to perform. *(Deuteronomy 28:1-13, Matthew 5:17, John 8:28-29, Genesis 5:24, Hebrews 11:5, Amos 3:3)*
4. Faith is being fully persuaded of the truth, the prevailing circumstances notwithstanding, until the truth triumphs. *(Romans 4:17-21)*
5. Faith is an unstoppable force in the world of the spirit. Whatever cannot stop the way against God cannot stop the way against faith from accomplishing its mission. *(Isaiah 43:13)*
6. Faith is our access to a world of unlimited possibilities. *(Mark 5:36, Matthew 9:27-29)*
7. There is no irreversible case with faith. *(John 11:39-45)*

## Note that

1. It is faith that puts believers in command of the supernatural. *(Mark 9:23, Matthew 8:8-10, 13, Matthew 15:22-28)*
2. The hand of God that commands the supernatural can only be brought to bear by faith. *(Isaiah 53:1, John 14:10, Mark 16:20)*
3. Our level of command of the supernatural is defined by the level of faith at work in us. *(Acts 24:14, Philippians 4:13, Acts 19:11-12)*
4. It is by the word of our mouth that we command the supernatural, because our words are seeds. *(Luke 8:11, Genesis 8:22, Mark 11:23)*

## Why is faith so powerful while those who claim to believe are so powerless?

1. **Weak faith** cannot stand the wild storms of life. *(Matthew 8:26, Romans 4:19-20)*
2. **Love-void faith.** *(Romans 8:28, 1 Corinthians 13:1-2, Galatians 5:6)*
3. **Closed-mouth faith.** *(Psalm 81:10-14, Romans 10:10, Luke 21:15)*
4. **Idle or careless-word faith.** *(Matthew 12:36-37, Acts 14:3)*
5. **Defiled-conscience faith.** *(Titus 1:15, 1 Timothy 1:19, 1 Timothy 3:9)*
6. **Contradictory-action faith.** *(1 Samuel 2:3, James 2:18-24)*
7. **Hidden-doubt faith.** *(Jeremiah 17:9-10, Mark 11:23)*
